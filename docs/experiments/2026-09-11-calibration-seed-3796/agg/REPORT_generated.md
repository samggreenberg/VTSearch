## 4. Where does the shipped draw sit?
Percentile of draw 42 among its own block's draws, pooled over (block, band). A median near 0.5 means the pin is an ordinary sample; a systematic offset would be the one error no per-cell bootstrap could ever see.

## 6. What it means for every other study here
The spread is **not** an extra error bar to add to published numbers: a study that bootstraps over cells at the pinned draw already resamples cells that each carry their own realisation of it. What it is, is a floor that pairing cannot remove — two arms paired on (class, seed) are not paired on the split, because a different knob is a different trajectory — and a statement about where precision comes from.
