<!-- **a** — Now one band, up close. Earlier we put a range on a count with
     Clopper–Pearson. Here we use a posterior instead: a curve over every share
     the band could have right, high where it's believable. These add up across
     bands nicely, which we'll need in a minute. Before any picks it looks like
     this: anything's possible, and it's only worth about one pick, so real
     picks take over fast. -->

<!-- **b** — Two right out of five, and the curve gathers in the middle: likely
     9 to 79%. Clopper–Pearson on the same picks says 5 to 85%. It's wider
     because it's built to be safe in the worst case; whether our narrower one
     is still honest, we'll check at the end of the section. -->

<!-- **c** — But what we really want is a count, not a share. This band has 16
     photos. We've seen 5, 2 of them matches, and we fill in the other 11 from
     that curve. So the band likely holds 2 to 12 matches. -->

<!-- **d** — Give the same band a second round, one more right out of five: now
