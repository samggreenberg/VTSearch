### Finding 1

## Judging *regions* beats judging images

- Binary voting asks "is this image relevant?"
- Region voting asks "**which part**?"
- Wins on both boxed datasets — cost **and** ranking quality

<!-- The mechanism: a whole-image label on a mostly-irrelevant image is a
     mostly-wrong training signal. Regions localise the evidence. -->
