## Four iterations and an epilogue

1. **Cross-calibration** *— right in the limit, wild when positives are scarce*
2. **A label-free mixture cut** *— nothing to starve, but it never learns*
3. **Blend the two** *— the biggest single win, then: never hand over completely*
4. **Fuse them instead of averaging** *— the partially-labeled fit, shipped today*
5. **Epilogue: the cut-rule line** *— pre-registered, measured twice, closed*
