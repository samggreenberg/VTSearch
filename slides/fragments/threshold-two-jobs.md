# A detector *ranks*. A threshold *decides*.

## Twice: what you keep, and what you are asked about next.

<!-- The one-sentence version of the slide before it; land it and move on.
     The whole deck lives in the gap between ranking and deciding.

     Training produces an ordering of the corpus. The user never experiences
     an ordering. What they experience is a decision: this came back, that did
     not; you were asked about this, not about that. Both of those are the
     threshold, and both of them are estimated — from the user's own votes,
     of which there may be six.

     Say the second job out loud one more time, because it is the part that
     surprises people and it is the reason the numbers later in the deck move
     as much as they do. A better threshold does not only fix the final set.
     It changes which items get shown, which changes which votes get cast,
     which changes the model, which changes the whole trajectory. The
     threshold compounds. -->
