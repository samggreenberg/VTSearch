<!-- **a** — Quick stats detour. A band of eight; pick five at random, four
     are right. Obvious guess is 80%, but it's five picks. How sure are we? -->

<!-- **b** — The picks settle most of it: four right, one wrong, three nobody's
     seen. All wrong or all right, that's four to seven of the eight: 50 to 88%.
     Even four of eight gives these picks one time in fourteen; too often to
     rule out. -->

<!-- **c** — Same picks from a band of 32: twenty-seven unseen, so chance sets
     the low end. Under 12 right, four or more of five comes up less than one
     time in twenty. -->

<!-- **d** — So 38 to 97%. That's Spot Check's range: exact, for a band whose
     size it knows. -->

<!-- **e** — The textbook range, Clopper-Pearson, assumes an endless list: 34
     to 99%. Spot Check's first bands are eight, eight and sixteen, so knowing
     the size buys a lot. -->
