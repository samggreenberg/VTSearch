<!-- **a** — First idea: don't sample the pile as one lump. Cut it into bands,
     the same way Spot Check did: the top 8, the next 8, then 16, doubling as
     you go. Small bands at the top, where the ranking changes fastest. This
     carries on below the line too, but for now let's stay above it. -->

<!-- **b** — Five random picks in each band. Four of five right in the top
     band, four in the next, two in the 16 just above the line. Random inside
     each band means the detector gets no say in what we see. -->

<!-- **c** — Now each band's share right, times its size: 6.4 matches in each
     band, 19.2 out of 32, so 60% right. Lump all fifteen picks together and
     you'd get 67%, because the top half of the set got ten of the fifteen
     picks. Weighting by band size undoes that. The truth we planted is 56%. -->

<!-- **d** — And the bands pay off twice. The same fifteen picks read every
     depth: the top 8 are 80% right, the top 16 also 80%, the top 32, 60%. So
     the test can tell us about other lines without asking for a single extra
     vote, and that comes back at the end. -->
