<!-- **a** — Back to the start. Remember this: the detector, its looser and
     tighter cuts, and its question right on the line, where it couldn't tell.
     Picking that question was the line's second job. -->

<!-- **b** — But another answer is to ask further in. Once there are radios,
     where you want the cut and where the model can't tell aren't the same
     place anymore. -->

<!-- **c** — Back to our fit, and its other number: each photo's chance of
     being a match. At the middle radio, the line sits where that chance is
     about 0.43. -->

<!-- **d** — Autopilot asks where it hits even odds: going down from the top,
     the first photo that's a coin toss. Here that's just inside the line. -->

<!-- **e** — The other radios move the line, up to 0.87 for beta of a quarter
     and down to 0.05 for beta of 4, where we'd be asking about near-certain
     non-matches. Even odds doesn't move: it's about the fit, not what you want
     back. -->

<!-- **f** — And the loop closes: the answer becomes a vote, the model
     retrains, and the fit gets redrawn. Asking here finds more matches than
     asking at the line. -->
