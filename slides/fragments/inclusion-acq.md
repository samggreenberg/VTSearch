<!-- **a** — Back to the start: the detector, its two cuts, and its question
     right on the line. Picking that question was the line's second job. -->

<!-- **b** — But we could ask one further in. The line is the best cut for
     what comes back right now; the best question is the one the detector
     learns the most from. Those aren't the same place. -->

<!-- **c** — So we use our fit's other number, each photo's chance of a match.
     Autopilot asks at even odds: the first photo from the top that's a coin
     toss. Here, just inside the line, which sits at about 0.42. -->

<!-- **d** — The other radios move the line, up to 0.86 or down to 0.05. Even
     odds stays put, so all three ask this same photo: it's about the fit, not
     what you want. -->

<!-- **e** — And the loop closes: vote, retrain, redraw. Asking here finds more
     matches than asking at the line. -->
